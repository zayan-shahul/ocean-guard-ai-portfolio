AI-powered underwater marine debris and anomaly detection | Ocean Guard AI |




⸻

🌊 01. Project Overview

Ocean Guard AI is an AI-driven marine technology initiative designed to advance underwater monitoring through intelligent sonar analysis, automated debris identification, and environmental intelligence.

The project explores the integration of Artificial Intelligence, side-scan sonar technology, and advanced image-processing techniques to transform conventional underwater inspection into a more systematic and technology-driven process.

Our approach focuses on reducing repetitive manual interpretation, improving underwater object analysis, and exploring innovative sensing methods for emerging marine pollution challenges.

Our Vision: To develop intelligent technologies that contribute to cleaner oceans and more efficient underwater environmental monitoring.

⸻

⚠️ 02. The Challenge

Underwater marine environments contain various forms of debris and anomalies that can be difficult to identify through conventional inspection methods.

Major challenges include:

* Extensive manual interpretation of underwater sonar imagery.
* Time-consuming identification of submerged objects.
* Difficulty distinguishing marine debris from natural underwater structures.
* Limited automation in conventional underwater inspection.
* Complex underwater environments affecting detection accuracy.
* Increasing concerns regarding microplastic contamination.

These challenges highlight the importance of developing intelligent systems that can support efficient and consistent underwater analysis.

⸻

💡 03. Our Proposed Innovation

Ocean Guard AI explores an integrated technological approach combining AI-powered image analysis, underwater sensing, and intelligent environmental monitoring.

A. AI-Powered Marine Debris Detection

Utilising side-scan sonar imagery and Artificial Intelligence to identify and classify potential underwater marine debris.

B. Intelligent Underwater Anomaly Detection

Applying advanced image-processing techniques to recognise unusual underwater structures and patterns, supporting systematic marine environment analysis.

C. Microplastic Detection — Our Research Innovation

Microplastic contamination is an emerging environmental concern that requires innovative monitoring approaches.

Ocean Guard AI explores the possibility of integrating specialised acoustic sensing techniques with AI-assisted analysis to investigate microplastic-related signatures.

This research direction aims to explore the potential of advanced sensing technologies for identifying and monitoring microplastic contamination, subject to experimental validation.

D. Automation of Underwater Analysis

Reducing repetitive manual interpretation through intelligent analysis, structured identification, and automated data processing.

🔬 05. System Workflow

The proposed system follows a structured analytical process:

1. Data Acquisition: Collection of underwater sonar imagery.
2. Pre-processing: Preparation and enhancement of collected data.
3. AI-Based Analysis: Intelligent examination of underwater imagery.
4. Object Identification: Recognition of potential marine debris and anomalies.
5. Microplastic Research: Investigation of specialised sensing and analytical techniques.
6. Visualisation: Presentation of analysis results.
7. Environmental Intelligence: Generating useful insights for underwater monitoring.
